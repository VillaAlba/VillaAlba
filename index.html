Lejlighed 401 ligger i Villa Alba — bygget i 2019 og beliggende i den gamle bydel i Antibes. En by-lejlighed i en af Rivieraens hyggeligste byer, med gå-afstand til alt fra shopping, caféer, restauranter og strande.

Apartment 401 is located in Villa Alba — built in 2019 and situated in the old town of Antibes. A city apartment in one of the Riviera's most charming towns, within walking distance of everything from shopping and cafés to restaurants and beaches.


Lejligheden består af 2 soveværelser, stue/køkken, 2 badeværelser og 2 altaner.

The apartment comprises 2 bedrooms, an open-plan living room and kitchen, 2 bathrooms and 2 balconies.

Antibes & omegn

Antibes & beyond

Antibes er en af Rivieraens bedst bevarede byer med en middelalderlig gammel bydel, en livlig havn, lange sandstrande og det berømte Picasso-museum. Fra Villa Alba er du kun få minutters gang fra det hele.

Antibes is one of the best-preserved towns on the Riviera, with a medieval old town, a vibrant harbour, long sandy beaches and the famous Picasso Museum. From Villa Alba you are just minutes on foot from everything.


Nice Côte d'Azur Lufthavn er kun 20 minutter væk i bil, og med tog er du i Monaco på 30 minutter og i Cannes på blot 15.

Nice Côte d'Azur Airport is only 20 minutes away by car, and by train you can be in Monaco in 30 minutes and in Cannes in just 15.

🛒 Indkøb

🛒 Shopping

Marché Provençal — det daglige provençalske marked — holder til 5 min. til fods. Supermarché og boulangerie i umiddelbar nærhed.

The Marché Provençal — the daily fresh market — is 5 minutes on foot. Supermarché and boulangerie are also close by.

🏖️ Strande

🏖️ Beaches

Plage de la Gravette og Plage du Ponteil er begge inden for 10 minutters gang — to af Antibes' smukkeste familiestrande.

Plage de la Gravette and Plage du Ponteil are both within a 10-minute walk — two of Antibes' most beautiful family beaches.

🎨 Attraktioner

🎨 Attractions

Musée Picasso, Vieille Ville (den gamle bydel), Cap d'Antibes, Juan-les-Pins og Monaco er alle nemme udflugtsmål.

Musée Picasso, Vieille Ville (the old town), Cap d'Antibes, Juan-les-Pins and Monaco are all easy day trips.

From simple harbour cafés to Michelin-starred restaurants — Antibes caters to all levels. We are happy to share our personal favourites on arrival.

Transport til Antibes

Getting to Antibes

✈️

Fly

By air

Nice Côte d'Azur Lufthavn (NCE) er den nærmeste lufthavn — kun 20 minutters kørsel fra Villa Alba. Der er direkte forbindelser fra København med SAS og Norwegian. Fra lufthavnen kan du tage taxa, leje bil eller tage bussen (linje 200) direkte til Antibes.

Nice Côte d'Azur Airport (NCE) is the nearest airport — only 20 minutes' drive from Villa Alba. There are direct connections from Copenhagen with SAS and Norwegian. From the airport you can take a taxi, rent a car or take the bus (line 200) directly to Antibes.

🚆

Tog

By train

Antibes har sin egen togstation med hyppige forbindelser langs kysten. Fra Nice er rejsetiden ca. 25 minutter, fra Cannes kun 15 minutter. Stationen ligger 10 minutters gang fra Villa Alba.

Antibes has its own train station with frequent connections along the coast. From Nice the journey is about 25 minutes, from Cannes just 15 minutes. The station is a 10-minute walk from Villa Alba.

🚗

Bil

By car

Fra Nice følger du motorvejen A8 mod vest og tager afkørslen Antibes. Rejsetiden er ca. 30 minutter. Villa Alba har privat parkering til to biler inden for matriklen. Bemærk at der kan være kø i højsæsonen.

From Nice, follow the A8 motorway westbound and take the Antibes exit. Journey time is approximately 30 minutes. Villa Alba has private parking for two cars within the property. Please note that traffic can be heavy during peak season.
